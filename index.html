Artificial Stupidity

The unofficial discipline dedicated to explaining why "smart" systems confidently do the dumbest things possible.

What is it?

Artificial Stupidity describes situations where technology, automation, or AI behaves in an illogical, inefficient, or hilariously wrong way — usually while insisting it is correct.

Classic Symptoms

  • Understands complex tasks, fails simple ones
  • Follows rules with zero common sense
  • Gives long answers that solve nothing
  • Creates new problems while fixing old ones

Root Causes

  • Bad or biased training data
  • Over-automation
  • Poor system design
  • Lack of real-world context

Real-Life Examples

  • Autocorrect changing correct words
  • Support bots looping the same answer
  • Apps with 10 steps for a 1-step action
  • AI confidently hallucinating facts
